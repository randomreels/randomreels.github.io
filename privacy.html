Random Reels

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

Random Reels ("the app") is made by Lakhvir Burm, Aldergrove, British Columbia, Canada. Contact: randomreels287@gmail.com.

No account needed

The app has no sign-up and never asks for your name, email, phone number, contacts or precise location.

What stays on your iPhone

Your settings, saved videos, watch history and World Passport (the countries you have watched videos from) are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

What our server receives

To pick videos, the app sends our server a random app ID created on your device, your feed filters (tab, language, country, category, date) and the IDs of videos you have already been shown, so you don't see them again. Our server also sees your IP address, as every internet service does. We use this only to run the feed, apply plan limits (for example daily counts on the Free plan) and keep the service secure. We keep daily counts for one day and server logs for no longer than 30 days. We don't sell this data or use it for advertising.

YouTube

Random Reels uses YouTube API Services. Videos play in YouTube's embedded player, which is run by YouTube; YouTube may collect information as you watch. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles data. Random Reels does not access your YouTube or Google account.

Ads (Free plan)

The Free plan shows ads from Google AdMob on break pages between videos. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interactions, to show and measure ads. Ads are non-personalized unless you turn on Personalized ads in Settings and allow tracking in Apple's prompt; where the law requires, you are asked for consent first. You can change this anytime in the app's Settings or in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Subscriptions

Payments are handled by Apple. We receive confirmation of your plan, not your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices and deletion

You can reset everything by deleting the app. To ask us to delete anything held on our server, email randomreels287@gmail.com with the date you used the app; we reply within 30 days. People in some places (for example the EU, the UK, California or Canada) have extra rights to access or delete data; contact us to use them.

Changes

We'll update this page and its date if the policy changes.
